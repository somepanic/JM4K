# JellyMod for Kodi (JM4K)

JellyMod lets you browse and play the films and series on your [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) media server (10.11 or
newer) from Kodi.

**Status: test builds.** JellyMod works with Jellyfin and is ready for testing, but it isn't released yet, so expect
rough edges. Music, photos and playlists are not included yet.

## What it does

- Sign in to your Jellyfin server with Quick Connect (code or QR code) or your username and password, find servers on
  your network, and switch users or servers.
- Browse Home's rows, your film and series libraries, and collections, and search titles and people.
- See each title's details, cast, seasons and episodes, and related titles.
- Play the original file whenever your device can, or stream a remux or transcode, with resume, skip intro and credits,
  seek-bar previews, chapters, theme music, subtitles, Stream Info and path mapping.
- Keep track of what you've watched, Continue Watching, Next Up and your favorites.
- Watch together: start or join a SyncPlay group from a title's menu or your user menu so everyone stays in step when
  someone pauses or skips.

## Where it comes from

JellyMod builds on [SiloMod for Kodi](https://github.com/somepanic/SM4K) (SM4K) and
[Plex Uno](https://github.com/FilHarr/plex-for-kodi) by FilHarr, which is a reworked fork of
[PlexMod for Kodi](https://github.com/pannal/plex-for-kodi) (PM4K) by pannal, which grew out of Plex's own Kodi
add-on. JellyMod keeps those screens and replaces the server layer with one that talks to Jellyfin.

## Installing

Not in a Kodi repository yet. To try a test build, install its zip file from Kodi's Add-ons screen (Install from zip
file). Releases will come through a Kodi repository later, and this page will say how to add it.
